Context

Why parents are leaving Nara Baby

Nara is a lovely app. The reasons we hear for switching are mostly about the new pricing, not the design.

💸

The free app is now paid

Nara's FAQ lists $9.99 per month or $99.99 one time. For many parents who picked Nara because it was free, that is the whole reason to look around.

🔒

Read-only after the trial

Once the 7-day trial ends without a subscription, you can still see and export your logs but you cannot add new ones. That is a hard stop at 3 a.m.

👆

Still tap-based logging

Nara uses timers and buttons. If you would rather say "fed 4oz, wet diaper" in one line, a chat-first tracker is faster.

🧮

Paying for basics

Feeds, sleep, and diapers are the core of any tracker. Several apps below still give you those for free.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Nara Baby alternatives

Quick answers to what parents ask us most.

Is Nara Baby still free?

No. Nara Baby is now a paid app. According to Nara's own FAQ it costs $9.99 per month or $99.99 one time for lifetime access, with a 7-day free trial that starts when you log your first activity. If you do not subscribe after the trial, the app becomes read-only: you can view and export your data, but you cannot add new entries.

What is the best free alternative to Nara Baby?

Pippy is our pick for most parents: AI chat logging, nap predictions, family sharing, and pediatrician-ready reports are all on the free tier. Baby Tracker by Nighp Software is the best free option if you prefer classic tap buttons, with free core tracking supported by ads. Huckleberry also has a free tier for basic logging and syncing between devices.

Can I export my Nara Baby data before switching?

Yes. Nara's FAQ says you can export your data even without a subscription: open the main Activity screen, tap the avatar next to your child's name, then tap Export Data to save a CSV file. Most trackers, Pippy included, do not import Nara files directly, so keep the CSV for your records and log the last few days in your new app to give it context.

Is Nara Baby lifetime worth $99.99?

It can be. One lifetime payment covers every child and every caregiver in your app family, with no extra tiers and no ads. If you plan to have more kids and you love Nara's design, lifetime is a fair deal. If you only need a tracker for the first year, free tiers like Pippy or Nighp cover the basics without paying.

Which Nara Baby alternative has AI?

Pippy lets you log by typing or speaking a normal sentence, like "she had 4oz at 2am", on the free tier. Huckleberry has AI logging inside its paid Plus plan. Baby Tracker by Nighp added on-device What's Next timing cues in its paid Plus plan.

Which Nara alternative is best for twins?

Nara itself is strong for twins because one payment covers all children. Among the alternatives, Baby Daybook supports multiple baby profiles and Huckleberry tracks multiple children. Pippy supports multiple babies on Premium at $49.99 per year.
