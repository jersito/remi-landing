Why offline matters with a newborn

Babies don't wait for good reception. The first feeds often happen in a hospital room where the phone barely connects. Then come the long drive to the grandparents, the flight home, the weekend away.

A tracker that only works online loses exactly those logs, or makes you write them on a napkin and type them in later. Pippy keeps them on the phone and uploads them when it can, so the timeline stays complete.

How It Works

What works offline, and what doesn't

No fine print. Here is exactly what Pippy does when there is no connection.

📴

Logs save on your phone

🔄

Sync by itself

When the connection is back, waiting entries upload on their own. Never twice, never dropped.

✏️

Edit while offline

Typed the wrong time? Entries waiting to sync can still be edited or deleted.

🛟

Server problems too

If our servers have a bad moment, your logs are kept the same way and a calm notice says what is going on.

FAQ

Offline questions parents ask us

Does Pippy work without internet?
Yes, for logging. From version 1.5.0, feeds, sleep, diapers and pumping you save with the log form or the feeding timer stay on your phone when there is no signal, and upload by themselves once you are back online. Each waiting entry says "Saved on this phone" until it has synced.
Can I edit or delete a log while I'm offline?
Yes. Entries that are still waiting to sync keep their edit and delete options, so you can fix a wrong time or amount right away.
Will an entry be saved twice when the connection comes back?
No. Each offline entry uploads once. It is never dropped and never doubled.
Does the chat work offline?
No. Chat logging and questions need a connection, because the AI runs on our server. While you are offline the message box says so and points you to the + button on the Timeline, which works with no signal.
Does my partner see offline logs?
Yes, as soon as your phone is back online. The entries sync to the shared timeline and everyone in your family sees them.
What happens if Pippy's servers have a problem?
The same thing as having no signal. Your logs are kept on the phone and sync later, and a calm notice at the top says what is going on. You are never sent back to the start or asked to set up your baby again.
Is it free?
Yes. Logging, the feeding timer, offline saving and family sharing are all in the free plan. Pippy is on iPhone and Android.
