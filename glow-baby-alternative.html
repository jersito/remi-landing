Context

Why parents look for Glow Baby alternatives

Glow Baby is a reasonable tracker with a devoted following, especially among parents who used Glow or Nurture during pregnancy. Still, a lot of parents try it and start looking around. Here is what we hear most often.

📣

Community mixed into tracking

Glow Baby surrounds the log with a social feed, articles, and daily digests. Some parents love it. Many just want to log a feed at 3 a.m. without scrolling past community posts to get there.

💸

The ~$59.99/year premium

Longer history and the better insights live in Glow Premium, priced near Huckleberry Plus territory. For a menu-based tracker without AI logging, plenty of parents decide that is not where their subscription budget goes.

🧭

Menu-based logging, no AI

Every entry is taps through forms. Apps built in the last few years let you type or say what happened in plain words and turn it into a structured entry.

🔒

Privacy history of the suite

The wider Glow suite drew a 2020 settlement with the California Attorney General over security flaws in its fertility app. Glow says the issues were fixed, but for some parents, baby data feels like the wrong place to extend trust.

Best completely free, zero paywalls

Nara Baby

Free tier: fully unlockedPremium: not requirediOS & Android

Nara Baby is the purest free answer to Glow Baby. Every core feature is unlocked from day one: sleep, feeding, diapers, growth, pumping, and a genuinely good developmental milestone tracker. There is no paywall, no "upgrade to unlock insights" nagging, and no sponsored content sitting next to your baby's data.

The UI is clean, logging is button-based and fast, and family sharing works across devices without a subscription. Where Glow Baby asks $59.99 per year for its full picture, Nara asks nothing.

The catch is what it does not try to do. There is no AI or conversational entry, no community, and sleep predictions are lighter than Huckleberry's. If you resent paying for baby tracking at all, Nara is the app to install first.

Best for: budget-conscious parents, subscription haters, milestone trackers Not for: anyone who wants AI logging or a community

Pros

  • Strong milestone tracking for a free app
  • Works across devices for partner sharing

Cons

  • No AI chat logging
  • Sleep predictions are basic
  • Reports are functional, not beautiful
  • Smaller team, updates can be slower
Verdict: If zero dollars is the budget and you do not need AI, Nara Baby is the one. The best free tracker in the category.
3
Best for sleep science & nap prediction

Huckleberry

Free tier: basicPlus: $58.99/yriOS & Android

Huckleberry is the category leader Glow Baby is usually compared against, and on sleep it earns that position. Its SweetSpot algorithm predicts the next nap window with an accuracy and polish nothing else in the category matches, and Huckleberry Premium adds consultant-designed sleep plans for families deep in a sleep regression.

Where Glow Baby spreads itself across tracking, community, and content, Huckleberry concentrates on sleep and does it well. The tradeoff is pricing: the features people install it for, including SweetSpot and AI logging, sit inside Huckleberry Plus at $58.99 per year, and the free tier logging is menu-heavy.

If you are leaving Glow Baby specifically because your baby's sleep is the problem you are trying to solve, Huckleberry is the strongest pick on this list. If you mostly need fast everyday logging, see Pippy or Nara first. We wrote a full guide to Huckleberry alternatives if you want that comparison in depth.

Best for: sleep-troubled households, nap schedule optimizers Not for: free-tier seekers, feed-and-diaper-first parents

Pros

  • SweetSpot nap prediction is the best in the category
  • Consultant-designed sleep plans on Premium
  • Free multi-child tracking
  • Large content library on sleep regressions

Cons

  • Headline features need Plus at $58.99/yr
  • Menu-heavy logging on the free tier
  • Sleep-first framing can feel like overhead
  • Premium with consulting is $119.99/yr
Verdict: The best sleep tracker, full stop. Worth the subscription if sleep is your battle. Overkill if it is not.
4
Best for detailed logging, 20+ activities

Baby Daybook

Free tier: generousPremium: ~$29.99/yriOS & Android

Baby Daybook is the "log absolutely everything" choice. It supports more than 20 tracked activities, from feeds and sleep through medicine, temperature, baths, and solids, which is well beyond what Glow Baby covers. It is an App Store favorite with over 2 million users.

The free tier is generous and premium is around $29.99 per year, the cheapest subscription in this comparison and roughly half of Glow Premium. Data-inclined parents get rich statistics, trend graphs, and CSV export.

The tradeoff is density. The UI can feel crowded until you configure which categories you care about, and there is no AI logging. If you want maximum data on your baby's day at the lowest subscription price, it is the best pick here.

Best for: data-driven parents, detailed medical tracking Not for: minimalists, grandparents logging handoffs

Pros

  • 20+ tracked activities, widest coverage here
  • Cheapest premium in this list (~$29.99/yr)
  • Strong statistics and CSV export
  • App Store Editor's Choice pedigree, 2M+ users

Cons

  • Dense UI, steeper setup curve
  • No AI chat logging
  • Free sharing is limited
  • Overkill for parents who only want basics
Verdict: Maximum tracking depth for the lowest subscription. Plan on 15 minutes of setup.

Best simple one-time buy

Baby Tracker (Nighp Software)

Free tier: generous~$4.99 one-timeiOS & Android

Baby Tracker by Nighp Software is the anti-subscription pick. It has been around for years, works offline, and covers feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping, growth, and milestones with fast tap-based logging. A small one-time unlock removes the limits, and then you never think about pricing again.

You give up predictions, insights, and AI entirely. This is a logbook, and proudly so.

Best for: one-time-purchase fans, offline-first households, NICU tracking Not for: anyone who wants predictions or insights

6
Best for daycare & caregiver handoffs

Baby Connect

Trial, then ~$4.99/moiOS, Android, Web

Baby Connect is the app a lot of daycares hand out. If your care situation involves more than two adults, its multi-caregiver model is the most polished in this comparison. Each adult gets their own login, and the timeline merges everyone's entries in near real time. It also has something neither Glow Baby nor most competitors offer: a real web dashboard.

It is subscription only, usually around $4.99 per month after a short trial, and the interface is functional but dated. There is no AI logging and no community. It exists to coordinate care across several adults, and at that job it is excellent.

If you are a solo parent or a two-parent household leaving Glow Baby, this is probably more machinery than you need. If a daycare or nanny is in the picture, it moves up the list fast.

Best for: daycare users, 3+ caregivers, web-dashboard seekers Not for: solo parents, free-tier seekers

Pros

  • Best-in-class multi-caregiver sharing
  • Full web dashboard, not just mobile
  • Widely used by daycares
  • Tracks well past baby years

Cons

  • No free tier, short trial then paid
  • No AI chat logging
  • Utilitarian, dated UI
  • Subscription costs add up over 2+ years
Verdict: If your daycare already uses it, pick it and move on. Otherwise the subscription-only model is a hard sell.

Honest Take

Where Glow Baby still wins

We are the team behind Pippy, so you might expect us to talk Glow Baby down. We will not. Here is where it beats every app above, including ours.

Stick with Glow Baby if

  • You used Glow or Nurture through pregnancy. The continuity is real: your history, due date, and the familiar interface carry straight over. No alternative replicates that.
  • The community is part of your support system. Asking other parents questions inside the app is something no tracker on this list offers.
  • You like one ecosystem for the whole journey. Cycles, pregnancy, and baby under one account and one design language.
  • The daily content digest works for you. Glow's week-by-week development content is decent, and it is where you already read it.

Switch to an alternative if

  • You open the app to log, and the feed is in your way
  • You want AI chat logging that is fast at 3 a.m. one handed
  • You do not want a $59.99 per year subscription for insights
  • Family sharing with a partner or nanny is a daily need
  • You would rather keep baby data out of a social product

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Glow Baby alternatives

Quick answers to what parents ask us most often.

What is the best Glow Baby alternative in 2026?

For most parents, Pippy is the best Glow Baby alternative. It focuses purely on tracking with no community feed, and its AI chat logging, nap window predictions, family sharing, and pediatrician-ready reports are all free. Nara Baby is the runner up if you want zero paywalls. Huckleberry is the pick if sleep science is your priority.

Is there a completely free alternative to Glow Baby?

Yes. Nara Baby is fully free with no paywalls and no ads, covering sleep, feeding, diapers, growth, and milestones. Pippy is free for its core features including AI chat logging and family sharing, with premium only needed for multiple babies, photos, and unlimited history.

Why do parents switch away from Glow Baby?

The reasons we hear most: the busy interface that mixes a community feed with tracking, the ~$59.99/year premium for insights and longer history, family sharing that feels secondary, and menu-based logging with no AI input. Parents who came from the Glow pregnancy apps often stay for continuity. Parents starting fresh tend to prefer a lighter dedicated tracker.

Can I move my Glow Baby data to another app?

Most trackers (including Pippy, Nara Baby, and Baby Daybook) do not import Glow Baby data directly. The practical approach is to manually log the last few days of feeds, sleep, and diapers in the new app so you have recent context, then start fresh.

Does any Glow Baby alternative have AI logging?

Pippy is the only app in this comparison with free AI chat logging: type or say "she had 4oz at 2am" and it becomes a structured entry. Huckleberry offers AI logging too, but behind Huckleberry Plus at $58.99/year.

Which Glow Baby alternative is best for sleep tracking?

Huckleberry, thanks to its SweetSpot nap prediction algorithm, though the best features require Plus. Pippy includes free nap window predictions on the free tier, which covers what most parents actually use. For neutral guidance on infant sleep, the AAP's HealthyChildren.org sleep pages are a good starting point.

I used Glow or Nurture during pregnancy. Should I still switch?

If the pregnancy-to-baby continuity and the community matter to you, staying with Glow Baby is reasonable, and we say so plainly above. If you mostly open the app to log feeds and sleep quickly, a dedicated tracker like Pippy or Nara Baby will feel faster every single day, and the switch takes minutes.

Which Glow Baby alternatives work on Android?

All six apps in this guide ship native Android apps: Pippy, Nara Baby, Huckleberry, Baby Daybook, Baby Tracker by Nighp Software, and Baby Connect.
